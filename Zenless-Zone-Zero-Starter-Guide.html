New Proxy Briefing

Zenless Zone Zero: what to know before your first Hollow dive

A day-one cheat sheet: what to claim, how the combat actually works, and the one early mistake worth skipping.

Right now: Version 3.1 lands July 29 — the second anniversary

"The Long Goodbye" launches July 29, 2026, marking two years of ZZZ. Every Proxy, new or existing, gets a free limited S-rank agent of their choice on login — Jane, Soldier 0 – Anby, Hugo, Trigger, or Lucia — plus their exclusive W-Engine, on top of 1,600 Polychrome and 20 Signal Searches. Nobody misses out by starting now: the rewards land whenever you next log in after the update, not just for accounts created that day. The only reason to hold off starting entirely is if you'd rather your very first session double as the anniversary login, purely for the novelty of it.

Chapter 01

Day one moves

Handle these first — they're either free or time-limited.

  • Correction

    The free S-rank pick isn't automatic — and Ellen Joe isn't in it

    You unlock a free S-rank agent after reaching 300 lifetime pulls on the standard "Stable Channel" banner, choosing from that banner's roster — agents like Lycaon, Grace, Soldier 11, or Nekomata. Ellen Joe is a limited-banner exclusive and isn't part of this pool; you pull for her separately. Lycaon is the strongest all-around pick since his stun kit supports almost any team. That 300-pull selector is separate from the one-time anniversary S-rank gift arriving with Version 3.1 — you get both, and they draw from different rosters.

  • First pulls come discounted

    New accounts get reduced-cost early pulls on the standard banner with a guaranteed S-rank inside them — an easy way to build your first roster cheaply.

  • You start with a full team

    The Cunning Hares — Anby, Nicole, and Billy — are handed to you at the start, and Pyrois joins for free through the Version 3.0 story.

  • Two currencies, two rules

    Save Polychrome for limited character banners, not the standard one. Bangboo companions pull on their own separate currency and cost nothing extra to chase.

Chapter 02

How to actually play

The habits that make the combat click.

  • Stun, then burst

    Break the enemy's stagger gauge, then dump damage in the daze window that follows. That single rhythm is most of the combat loop.

  • Swap between all three agents

    A team is one combo, not three separate characters. Dodge and parry to chain swaps instead of sticking to a single main.

  • Pace it your way

    Do dailies when you want to play, skip them when you don't — the rewards are small enough that it's not worth treating as a job.

  • There's no 100% checklist

    It's a style-and-combat playground more than a completionist game. Play for the verbs — the fights and the swap-canceling — not a checklist.

Chapter 03

Watch out for

Two things that quietly waste your resources.

  • Don't farm gear early

    Skip serious Drive Disc farming until you're around Inter-Knot level 30 with a settled team. Gear built earlier usually gets scrapped once your build changes. Version 3.1 also speeds up the climb through Inter-Knot 20–50, so you'll reach that point sooner than earlier Proxies did.

  • Don't skip the Boopons

    Bangboo companions use their own free currency, separate from Polychrome — there's no reason to leave that pull sitting unused.
