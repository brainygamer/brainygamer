South Side Icons

Café Avellaneda

Viveros de Coyoacán

A quiet, beautiful morning walk through the local public nursery park alongside coyoacanenses jogging and dog-walking.

1:00 PM (Strict Window!)

The famous cobalt-blue house where Frida was born and died. Extremely strict 15-minute entry window (1:00 PM–1:15 PM) via Fever App QR codes.

Diego Rivera's volcanic stone masterpiece housing his immense pre-Hispanic art collection. Extremely unique vibe.

Late Afternoon

San Ángel Cobblestones & Jazz Terrace

Exploring the upscale, historical neighborhood of San Ángel and Plaza San Jacinto, then catching the free open-air jazz and local craft beers at Cineteca Nacional Terrace at 7:00 PM.

Floating Garden & Neighborhoods

8:00 PM

Late Night Tacos al Pastor: El Vilsito
