Amex Centurion Lounge Layover (Atlanta - ATL)

Exploring the Heart of Coyoacán

Take a gentle walk through Jardín Centenario, Plaza Hidalgo, and past the iconic coyote fountain. Enjoy legendary crab/shrimp tostadas at the back of Mercado de Coyoacán.

Historic Heart & Jazz Underground

Friday, June 5, 2026

Architecture & Music
8:30 AM

Uber to Centro Histórico

Commute north to the colonial heart of the city (approx. 30–40 minutes).

9:15 AM

Templo Mayor Aztec Ruins

Walk among the ruins and its multi-story artifact museum. (Entry approx. MX$90).

11:15 AM

Palacio de Bellas Artes

Behold legendary mural works by Diego Rivera, Siqueiros, and Orozco in the Art Deco masterpiece. (MX$95).

12:30 PM

Gourmet Feast: Mercado de San Juan

Famous food market known for exotic specialties, local cheeses, tapas, and incredible fresh seafood lunch counters.

2:00 PM – 6:30 PM

Torre Latinoamericana & La Ciudadela Crafts Market

Catch unparalleled 360-degree views of the city valley, then walk to the ultimate artisan market to browse and bargain for textiles, pottery, and gifts.

9:00 PM

Zinco Jazz Club

Live jazz in an underground former bank vault. Superb acoustic space, swanky ambiance. Cover: ~MX$200–300 in CASH ONLY.

🔔 Crucial Checklist: Settle reservation via WhatsApp on June 3!

Frida, Diego & Southern Strolls

Saturday, June 6, 2026

South Side Icons
Morning Coffee

Café Avellaneda

Enjoy a slow single-origin pour-over and a light empanada at this tiny, legendary specialty neighborhood bar.

10:00 AM

Viveros de Coyoacán

Walk among majestic trees in the neighborhood nursery park, watching locals jog, stretch, and relax.

1:00 PM (Strict Entry!)

Casa Azul — The Frida Kahlo Museum

The famous cobalt-blue house. Super strict 15-minute entry window (1:00 PM–1:15 PM) via your Fever app QR code. No exceptions, don't be late!

2:30 PM

Museo Anahuacalli

Diego Rivera's volcanic stone temple designed to house his massive pre-Hispanic art collection. Extremely unique atmosphere.

4:30 PM – Evening

San Ángel Cobblestones & Jazz Terrace

Explore upscale cobblestone streets, and at 7:00 PM catch the free open-air jazz session and a local craft beer on the terrace of Cineteca Nacional.

📚 Bookstore Stop: Tlacuilo (Coyoacán's indie literary gem, highly curated and intimate).

Art, History & Urban Forests

Sunday, June 7, 2026

Culture & Parks
8:00 AM

Uber to Bosque de Chapultepec

Enter early into Section 2 of this massive park to enjoy its peaceful, forested walkways before Sunday crowds arrive.

9:30 AM – 1:30 PM

National Museum of Anthropology

Widely hailed as one of the best museums on Earth. Budget 3-4 hours to explore the Aztec Sun Stone and Mayan galleries. (MX$85–90).

1:30 PM

Lunch in Leafy Polanco or Roma Norte

Relax on a sidewalk café and enjoy world-class architecture and dining.

3:00 PM

Polanco Modern Art Loop

Visit the eye-catching, aluminum-sheathed Museo Soumaya (always free), and the striking contemporary galleries of Museo Jumex next door (also free!).

Water Canals & Trendy Strolls

Monday, June 8, 2026

Floating Garden & Neighborhoods
7:30 AM

El Jarocho Fuel Up

Morning coffee de olla and pan dulce standing under the canopy of your local neighborhood institution.

9:00 AM – 11:00 AM

Xochimilco Floating Canals (The Serene Way)

Rent a private, colorful wooden trajinera boat. Launch from **Embarcadero Cuemanco**—the quietest, eco-friendly pier—to drift past ancient Aztec chinampas with birds and mist. Total cost: MX$1,500 for 2 hours (CASH ONLY!).

12:15 PM

Roma Norte Exploration

Lunch at Mercado de Medellín (highly authentic and un-touristy), then stroll through Cafebrería El Péndulo—a stunning multi-level bookstore and garden-forest.

4:30 PM

Parque México Condesa Stroll

Walk the gorgeous Art Deco circular paths, look at the dog-training clubs, and check out the historic building facades.

8:00 PM

Late Night Tacos al Pastor: El Vilsito

An auto repair shop by day, it turns into a legendary tacos al pastor hotspot with towering trompos after 9 PM. Absolute CDMX legend. (CASH ONLY!).

Murals, Peacocks & Last Strolls

Tuesday, June 9, 2026

South Side Icons & Prep
Morning

UNAM Ciudad Universitaria

Witness the giant, iconic stone mosaic murals by Juan O'Gorman covering the Biblioteca Central and explore the volcanic, minimalist landscape of Espacio Escultórico.

11:30 AM (Confirmed Tix!)

Museo Dolores Olmedo

Walk through the spectacular colonial hacienda. View unparalleled paintings by Diego Rivera and Frida Kahlo, surrounded by roaming peacocks and hairless Xoloitzcuintli dogs. (Reopened May 30!).

Late Afternoon

Logistics & Souvenirs

Stop by **Café Negro** to buy single-origin bags of roasted Mexican beans to take home. Settle any incidentals at Maria Del Alma House and pack your bags.
