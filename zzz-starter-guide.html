Ch.01Signal check

Day one moves

Claim these before you do anything else.

  • Start here 01

    Free S-rank selector

    Pick Lycaon (a stunner, endlessly flexible) or Ellen Joe (an Ice DPS). Either one anchors a real team — you can't misfire here.

  • 02

    Discounted first pulls

    The standard banner takes 20% off your first 50 pulls and guarantees an S-rank agent somewhere inside them.

  • 03

    Free starter team

    The Cunning Hares — Anby, Nicole, Billy — are handed to you. Pyrois joins free from the story on top of that.

  • 04

    Currency rule

    Hoard Polychrome for limited banners, never the standard one. Bangboo are 100% free to pull with their own currency.

Ch.02Field manual

How to actually play

The habits that make the combat click.

  • 01

    Stun, then burst

    Break the enemy's stagger gauge, then dump everything in the daze window. That single rhythm is the whole loop.

  • 02

    Swap-cancel everything

    All three agents are one combo. Dodge and parry to chain between them — don't just main a single character.

  • 03

    Pace it your way

    Do the dailies when you feel like playing, skip them guilt-free when you don't. Don't let it turn into a job.

  • 04

    Adjust your appetite

    There's no map to 100%. It's a style-and-combat playground — play it for the verbs, not the checklist.

Ch.03Caution

Watch out for

One trap that quietly wastes hours.

  • Don't farm gear early

    Skip Drive Disc farming until Inter-Knot level 30 with a locked-in team, or you'll pour resources into builds you're only going to scrap.
